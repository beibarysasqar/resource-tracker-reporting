# Welcome guide

Welcome to the resource planning team. This guide is short on purpose. It covers what we do, which files matter and what your first week looks like. Everything else is in the SharePoint library.

## What the team does

We keep track of who works on which project and how many hours. Project managers send us requests for people, we check who has free capacity and record the assignment in the resource tracker. Once a month we report planned hours against actual hours and flag people who are overloaded.

## The resource tracker

The tracker is one Excel workbook with three sheets:

- employees: id, name, department, role, grade, monthly capacity in hours
- projects: id, name, department, start and end month, status, budget in hours
- assignments: one row per employee, project and month with planned and actual hours

Things to know:

- Hours are monthly. When we talk about weekly load we divide the month by 4.
- A person is counted as overloaded in a month when the weekly load is above 40 hours.
- Part-time colleagues have a capacity of 80 hours a month, full-time 160.
- Never type over formulas in the summary sheets. New data goes into the assignments sheet only.

## Monthly cycle

1. Project managers confirm actual hours for the previous month.
2. We refresh the tracker with Power Query.
3. We check the pivot tables against the summary file.
4. We update the resource report slides and send them to the team lead for review.

## Tools

- Excel: the tracker and all summary tables
- SharePoint: the document library. Every document has a type, an owner and a status.
- Planner: the team board with the columns To do, In progress, Review, Done
- PowerPoint: the monthly resource report
- Word: templates, policies and guides

## Your first week

- Day 1: accounts, access, this guide, intro call with the team
- Day 2: walk through the tracker with your buddy
- Day 3: read the time logging policy and the overtime guidelines
- Day 4: refresh a copy of the tracker yourself
- Day 5: take your first task from the Planner board

The onboarding checklist has the full list of steps with owners.

## Who to ask

- Access and accounts: team lead
- Tracker, formulas, Power Query: your buddy
- Questions about a specific project: the project manager named in the project brief

## A few rules

- Work on a copy when you practise. The main tracker is edited only during the monthly cycle.
- Name files as described in the document naming convention.
- If a number looks wrong, say so. It is better to ask twice than to send a wrong report.
